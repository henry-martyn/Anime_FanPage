02 · WHAT STORIES TEACH US

Three stories. Three different ideas.

01

Naruto · The People We Become

Pain does not have to decide who we become. The people who believe in us can change how we see ourselves.

Naruto — an orphan from birth, rejected by the very people whose lives were saved by his parents. 💔

Yet, he never let that pain consume him. Instead, he accepted it — understanding that the scars of the past may hurt, but what truly matters is what you choose to do now… and how you choose to look toward the future. ✨🌅

“What would I become after being hurt?”
02

One Piece · What Freedom Means

Freedom can mean something different to everyone. Our choices give meaning to the journey.

One Piece — a world where dreams are mocked, freedom is taken for granted, and people are often told that their ambitions are impossible. 🌊💔

Yet, it teaches us to fight for our freedom, to reclaim the power over our own lives, and to choose our own path. We are not meant to live under the will of those who seek to control us — we are our own people, free to chase our dreams, sail our own seas, and decide what our lives become. ✨🏴‍☠️🌅

“What does freedom mean to me?”
03

Horimiya · The People Around Us

People are often more complicated than they first appear. Connection can give someone a place to be themselves.

Horimiya — a story about first impressions, hidden sides of people, and the importance of being understood. 🌸💔

It teaches us that people are often more complicated than they first appear. We all have sides of ourselves that we hide from the world, and it is only through connection that we can find a place to be ourselves. ✨🌅

“Who really knows me?”
